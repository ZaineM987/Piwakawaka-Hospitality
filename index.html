Educator Notes: Hospitality & Pedagogy

This application integrates 50 Advanced Hospitality, Food & Beverage Concepts designed to introduce 4-to-6-year-olds to the culinary arts, empathy, teamwork, and kitchen safety.

By framing cooking as "flavor magic" and serving as the "art of making people smile," children learn the joy of sharing a meal and caring for others through Cognitive Load Theory (CLT):

  • Advanced Culinary Typography & Palette: Uses warm, high-contrast bistro aesthetics with Fraunces display headings and Plus Jakarta Sans body text for effortless reading.
  • Smartphone-First Experiential Scrolling: Root-level touch-action: pan-y compliance and a 45svh bottom buffer ensure that even lengthy multi-line answer options are never cut off on smaller phone screens.
  • Managing Intrinsic Load: Real-world trades concepts (Front vs. Back of House, Brigade teamwork, Zero Waste, Allergies, and Food Safety) are taught via relatable childhood metaphors.
  • Modality Effect & Storytime Pacing: The Text-to-Speech (TTS) channel reads both questions and individual answer options aloud at a gentle, child-friendly pace with natural breathing pauses and a top-bar speed toggle (🐢 Slow, 🐌 Extra Slow, 🐇 Normal).
